No excuses in here. Just the truth, in my own handwriting-ish font.

I know I'm the one who messed up. You're upset, and you have every right to be. I didn't think before I acted, and I hurt the one person I never want to hurt. I'm sorry — properly, from the bottom of my heart.

Forgive me this once? 🥺 I'll come with folded ears, your favourite chocolate, and a promise to do better.

بسمہ

Bisma — the name that means “smile”. Of course it does. Yours runs my entire world.

Couplet 01

خفا ہو تم تو دنیا کی خوشی ادھوری لگتی ہے

تمہاری اک ہنسی سے ہی تو صبح پوری لگتی ہے

✦

“When you're upset with me, every joy in this world feels incomplete —
one smile from you, and my whole morning becomes whole again.”

Couplet 02

کتابِ دل کے ہر ورق پہ اک ہی نام لکھا ہے

بھلا کیسے مٹاؤں میں، جسے خدا نے خود لکھا ہے

✦

“On every page of my heart, only one name is written —
how could I ever erase the one God wrote with His own hand?”
